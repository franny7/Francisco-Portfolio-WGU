Francisco's Career Goals

Resume Cover Letter

As you already know, my name is Francisco. I am currently a WGU undergrad student and I plan on graduating from this wonderful school. In this page, I want to talk a little bit about my short-term and long-term goal career goals.


Short-term Goals

My career goals are pretty straight forward. I currently work in IT but not exactly the position that I want to stay in. I am currently a Service Desk Analyst II, but with the help of this degree, I plan on transitioning into a Software Engineering Role within the same company. I am lucky to work for such a great company. I plan on taking full advantage of the opportunity that I have to be able to network within the organization. I have met a lot of intelligent and talented people that I can learn from and everyone is always so willing to help one another. I consider this a short-term goal because I know that I can accomplish this feat within the next 2 years.



Long-term Goals

I started this Software Engineering journey a few years ago and was lucky to gain real-world experience with a couple of friends of mine that are already in the field of Software Engineering. They are always willing to help and guide me through any questions I have. A couple of these friends of mine work at big companies; Amazon and Meta and are willing to refer myself and a group of friends to work at their company. After completing my short term goal, I plan on using that experience to further my career growth at one of these big companies. it is a goal of mine to contribute to these big companies and their software that are used by people all over the globe.


Conclusion

Career goals are only half of the equation though. With this growth in my career it leads me to what I most value in life, which is my family. I want to be able to provide for my family and give them the best life possible. I want to have a beautiful home with all the animals they would like and a big patch of grass where we can all run around and enjoy the space. I want to be able travel the world with them and create memories that they will never forget. I plan on taking trips to New York, Hawaii, Italy, and many other places. I have my goals lined up to be able to accomplish these things, and I will not stop until it has become a reality.
